# Restrict Member access to documents

Phase 4 lets Owner/Admin see all Documents in their Organization, but a Member sees only Documents they submitted or were explicitly assigned. A LINE group submission starts visible to Owner/Admin until a responsible Member is assigned; group presence alone does not grant document access. This costs an assignment step, but prevents a shared group or broad Organization membership from exposing private originals. Retrieval rechecks tenant and document authorization and uses short-lived signed access.
