# Separate monthly Document quota from structural Plan Over Limit

Phase 4 represents an exhausted monthly Document allowance separately from the existing structural Plan Over Limit state. Reaching or downgrading below a monthly Document limit blocks only new unique Document intake; authorized reads, exports and unrelated work continue. Structural Plan Over Limit still governs membership and LINE-group overages. This avoids turning a metered intake limit into an Organization-wide mutation lock while preserving the hard Document ceiling.
