# Count Trial Documents in a separate 14-day window

The one-time Business Trial permits 100 accepted unique Documents across its full 14 days, even when it crosses a calendar-month boundary. Documents accepted on Free before Trial activation do not consume the Trial allowance; Documents accepted during Trial still appear in the Organization's calendar-month Usage when Trial ends and the Free limit applies again. A separate Trial window avoids an accidental 200-Document allowance across a month boundary while preserving the monthly history used after expiry.
