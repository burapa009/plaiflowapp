# Keep accounting mapping decisions versioned and prospective

Phase 8 suggestions and exports must remain explainable after an Organization changes its mapping rules. An Owner/Admin-approved rule applies to new suggestions only; a one-document correction never silently becomes a reusable rule, and prior suggestions and exported snapshots retain the rule/template versions used at the time. A pending suggestion from an older rule version must be regenerated before approval; a change to the confirmed source revision also supersedes its derived suggestion. Archiving a referenced Vendor or category stops new matches without rewriting history. This favors reproducibility and auditability over automatically refreshing old results, which would change a previously reviewed accounting recommendation without a new human decision.
