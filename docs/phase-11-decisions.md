# Phase 11 — Subscription and Billing decisions

Status: Stripe payment design superseded by the [PromptPay amendment](./phase-11-promptpay-amendment.md), 2026-09-25. The Stripe-specific decisions below are historical. No billing feature is enabled.

## Settled

- Stripe was originally selected during the interview, then canceled on 2026-09-25. The payment provider remains undecided.
- The first paid checkout uses Stripe Hosted Checkout with cards and automatic renewal. PromptPay is deferred; a later PromptPay flow would need customer-initiated invoice payments rather than claiming automatic card-style renewal.
- Use fixed trusted server-side Plan/interval to Stripe Price ID mappings. The six-month option uses a native Stripe recurring Price with `interval=month` and `interval_count=6`; if the connected Stripe account cannot support it, do not sell the six-month option as an improvised one-time payment.
- Keep Free and the existing one-time 14-day Business Trial without requiring a card. Launch paid Starter and Business first. Accounting Firm Checkout opens only after the Phase 10 firm workspace passes its activation gates; its public price may be shown earlier with a clear unavailable purchase action.
- Define a fixed THB amount payable for each Plan and interval: monthly, six-month, and annual, using the approved table below. The public comparison shows the full charge, renewal interval, and effective monthly equivalent.
- Sell Accounting Firm as one flat subscription for the firm Organization, including its current ceilings of 5 staff seats, 20 client relationships, and 5 LINE groups for the firm Organization. Give the firm Organization its own grant of 1,000 accepted unique Documents per month, with paid-Plan carry. Its own purchased Scan Credits apply only to its own Documents. Every Client Organization uses its own Plan allowance, LINE group ceiling, and purchased Scan Credits. Do not add per-seat or per-client metering in the first version.
- Each Organization owns and pays for its own subscription, including Client Organizations served by an Accounting Firm. An Accounting Firm subscription does not pay for or replace a client's Plan.
- Only the Organization Owner can initiate checkout, change Plan or interval, cancel, resume, and view payment-sensitive history. Admin can view subscription status and renewal date without payment-sensitive details.
- Keep the current Free / Starter / Business monthly Document grants of 30 / 300 / 1,000 accepted unique Documents, alongside 1 / 3 / 10 active or reserved Memberships and 1 / 2 / 5 LINE groups. Unused paid-Plan Document allowance carries forward, and each monthly grant expires two years after it is issued. Free gets 30 new Documents each month but does not accumulate unused allowance. The Trial's separate 100-Document limit over its 14 days does not carry into another Plan. Document limits are hard; there is no automatic usage overage charge. No Plan or Trial includes scan pages in the first version: OCR needs purchased Scan Credits. Measure AI costs internally before adding an AI quota.
- A paid upgrade takes effect only after its immediate payment succeeds. Preview and disclose the credit for unused paid time and the amount due before Owner confirmation. Downgrades and moves to a shorter billing interval take effect at the current paid period's end.
- Buying a paid Plan during Trial starts that paid Plan after successful payment rather than waiting for Trial expiry. Cancellation stops renewal at the end of the paid period, with the end date visible; Owner can undo scheduled cancellation before that date.
- On failed renewal payment, retain the current paid Entitlements for a deterministic seven-day grace period. After grace, use Free Entitlements and the existing Plan Over Limit protections. Preserve all customer Documents and recovery/security access.
- Configure at most three automatic retry attempts within that seven-day grace period, separate from the initial failed charge. Notify the Owner after the first failure and offer a path to update the payment method and pay the outstanding invoice. The local seven-day deadline does not depend on Stripe's subscription-status timing.
- Refund requests are handled manually by support in the first version. Show available Stripe invoice and payment history with accurate labels. Thai VAT tax-invoice issuance remains a separate compliance workstream; a seller who is not VAT registered and not otherwise authorized cannot issue a VAT tax invoice. Automatically issue an ordinary PlaiFlow seller receipt after each verified successful subscription or Scan Credit payment, with a unique sequential receipt number, immutable payment snapshot, Owner download, and a retained copy for at least five years. Email that receipt to the verified Billing Contact; retry delivery with the same receipt number after an email failure. Confirm the actual seller's tax status and receipt format with a Thai accountant before live payment activation; a Stripe payment receipt alone is not assumed to satisfy the seller's receipt duty.
- Email the Organization Owner seven days before renewal and immediately after a failed payment. If the invoice remains unpaid, send further failure reminders on days three and six with the amount due and the seven-day grace deadline. Show the same status in the app. LINE billing notices are deferred to a later opt-in flow.
- After grace expires, configure Stripe to leave the Subscription `unpaid` and stop automatic retries. Allow payment recovery on that same Subscription for 30 more days; restore paid Entitlements only when Stripe confirms payment of the outstanding invoice. At the recovery deadline, cancel any still-unpaid Subscription; a later return requires a new Checkout. Verify Stripe invoice behavior in test mode so recovery does not create duplicate charges.
- An authenticated Owner may open Stripe Customer Portal for payment-method updates and available Stripe invoice/payment history. Seller receipts are downloaded separately in PlaiFlow. Plan and interval changes and cancellation remain controlled in PlaiFlow, with current-authorization checks and clear previews.
- Do not offer automatic refunds or coupon/promo codes in the first version. Publish a support-led, case-by-case refund policy for duplicate or erroneous charges before checkout.
- Keep Business marked as the recommended Plan. On mobile pricing cards, make the actual full charge and renewal interval more prominent than the effective monthly comparison. Show the end-of-access date in a clear cancellation confirmation; cancellation does not require a reason.
- The seller is not currently VAT registered. Display the approved THB price as the full charge without a VAT line, and do not issue or label a customer document as a Thai VAT tax invoice while unregistered. Revisit pricing and documents if VAT registration changes. Revenue Code section 86/13: https://www.rd.go.th/5208.html
- A change to a longer interval on the same Plan starts at the next renewal; a paid Plan upgrade may start immediately after the additional payment succeeds.
- Honor existing trusted manual Plan periods until their recorded end. Do not silently create a Stripe Subscription or back-charge; the Owner may choose Stripe checkout for a subsequent paid period.
- A verified full refund of the current paid period, or a finally lost payment dispute, removes paid Entitlements and applies Free/Plan Over Limit protections without deleting customer data. Partial refunds are reviewed manually and do not automatically change Entitlements.
- Require an explicitly supplied and verified billing-contact email before paid checkout, including for Owners who use LINE Login without a verified identity email.
- Before checkout, require the Owner to review the buyer name, address, and tax ID in the Organization Billing Profile. Snapshot those details on each seller receipt; profile edits affect only future receipts. Route correction requests for an already issued receipt through support rather than mutating its payment history. Seller identity and tax details come from verified server configuration, never client input.
- Offer optional scan-credit packs on every Plan, including Free. The revised packs supersede the earlier 70/150/400/1,200-credit offer: **500 pages ฿219; 800 pages ฿339; 1,800 pages ฿699; 5,000 pages ฿1,899**. One automatically scanned image or PDF page costs one credit, so a multi-page file costs the number of pages scanned. Do not offer unlimited OCR.
- Open paid Scan Credit Checkout only after OCR is enabled and passes representative accuracy, throughput, cost, and queue-fairness acceptance. Starter and Business may launch earlier because their paid Plans include zero OCR pages; Pricing and Checkout must state plainly when Scan Credit purchase is unavailable.
- Credit purchases are one-time card payments through Stripe Checkout initiated by the Organization Owner, with no automatic top-up. Unused credits roll into the next month without a monthly reset or expiry, remain usable on Free after a paid Subscription ends, and do not increase available Document Allowance.
- Reserve the full page count before OCR starts and consume it once only when the complete OCR result is published. Release the reservation after terminal failure. System retries and duplicate files consume no extra credits. A user-requested reprocess after a successful result requires confirmation and a new page-count reservation.
- On a verified full credit-pack refund, remove unspent credits from that purchase. Spent credits and partial refunds require manual support review; never delete the source Document as a refund side effect.
- Scan Credits apply to every document type supported by OCR; public copy says “เครดิตสแกนเอกสาร” rather than promising content classification as a receipt.
- Keep one public credit-pack price ladder. The example 500-page ฿79–99 and 1,000-page ฿149–179 top-ups are not separate first-version products because they undercut the stated 500-page ฿219 pack; revisit the whole ladder if lower prices are wanted.
- Credit balances belong to the Organization that owns the scanned Document. An Accounting Firm cannot pool one purchased balance across Client Organizations in the first version. A firm user acting for a client consumes that client's balance only with current delegated authorization.
- Retain the current 20-page PDF OCR limit for the first version. A 30-page example describes proportional credit counting, not supported file size; increasing the limit requires separate correctness and capacity evidence.
- If a newly accepted Document needs more Scan Credits than are available, retain the Document without a partial scan and show the number of missing credits. After a later credit purchase, the user selects and confirms which pending Documents to scan; newly accepted Documents may auto-scan when enough credits are available.
- Do not charge retrospectively for OCR results completed before Scan Credits launch. Viewing existing results is free; an explicit reprocess after a successful result consumes new credits with confirmation.
- Start accumulating paid Document allowance only when the Phase 11 carry policy is activated; do not manufacture a balance from earlier months. Grant new Document allowance monthly even when the Subscription bills every six months or year. Each monthly paid grant expires two years after issue; there is no additional balance cap, so a continuously unused paid balance can approach 24 monthly grants. Spend the earliest-expiring grant first.
- A voluntary move to Free stops accumulation and forfeits paid Document carry; purchased Scan Credits remain available on Free. A paid-to-paid downgrade keeps unexpired carried allowance up to 24 times the destination Plan's monthly grant. Preview any amount that would be lost before the Owner confirms the downgrade. Existing Documents are never deleted by these transitions.
- After a failed renewal passes its seven-day grace period, suspend the old paid Document carry so it cannot be spent while Free. If the outstanding payment is verified within 30 days after grace expiry, restore only grants that have not yet reached their original two-year expiry; do not issue grants for the unpaid gap. After that recovery window, or after a voluntary cancellation, the paid carry is forfeited.
- On an immediate mid-month paid upgrade, add only the difference between the old and new monthly Document grant for that calendar month; do not grant a second full monthly allowance. Preserve eligible unexpired carry subject to the new Plan's ceiling.
- Show the soonest-expiring paid Document allowance and its date in Billing. When a positive balance will expire within 30 days, show an in-app reminder; do not add an allowance-expiry email in the first version.
- Issue each monthly Document grant at 00:00 on the first day of the month in the Organization's timezone. A paid grant expires at 00:00 on the first day of the corresponding month two years later. Timezone changes affect future monthly periods only, not the issue or expiry timestamp of existing grants.
- Permit spending legitimately accumulated Document allowance without an undisclosed daily intake cap. Apply ordinary disclosed upload limits and fair per-Organization OCR queue scheduling; each OCR page still needs a purchased Scan Credit. Do not promise processing speed for large accumulated bursts until representative load tests pass.
- Do not add a new numerical AI or Export quota in Phase 11. Keep existing Plan capabilities and existing file/job limits; measure real costs before introducing metered prices or overages. When load requires work to wait, show an accurate queued or delayed state instead of a hidden quota denial.
- Verify Stripe webhook signatures, durably record each provider event ID once, acknowledge promptly, and process side effects asynchronously. Reconcile pending Checkout Sessions and past-due Subscriptions every 15 minutes, and active Subscriptions daily. During provider outages show the last verified local provider state and a sync-pending message; never grant new paid Entitlements from a browser redirect. Apply local deadlines such as the seven-day grace deadline deterministically, then repair verified mismatch after provider recovery.

## Current product baseline

- The code currently exposes Free, Starter, and Business Plan keys. Its displayed Starter prices are THB 199 monthly, 1,098.48 per six months, and 1,990 annually; Business prices are THB 499, 2,754.48, and 4,990. These are Early Access catalog values, not approved commercial prices.
- The existing one-time Business Trial lasts 14 days and allows 100 accepted unique Documents over the full trial. These limits and the current Plan ceilings now carry into the first commercial version.
- The Accounting Firm Plan currently has trusted manual periods but no public price or checkout. Its client and staff ceilings are 20 and 5. Firm access does not grant ownership of client data.

## Provider facts that affect the decisions

- Stripe recurring Prices can use `interval=month` with `interval_count=6`, so a native six-month Price is possible. Source: https://docs.stripe.com/api/prices/create
- Stripe PromptPay supports subscriptions only with `send_invoice`; it does not support Checkout in subscription mode. This makes automatic card renewal and customer-initiated PromptPay renewal different payment flows. Source: https://docs.stripe.com/payments/promptpay
- Stripe subscription changes can reset the billing date and produce immediate credits and charges when intervals change; the product must choose and disclose its own switching policy. Source: https://docs.stripe.com/api/subscriptions/update
- Stripe's monthly Thai tax invoice for Stripe fees is a document from Stripe to the merchant; it does not establish whether a Stripe invoice issued by the merchant to a PlaiFlow customer meets Thai tax-invoice requirements. Sources: https://support.stripe.com/questions/taxes-on-stripe-fees-for-thailand-based-businesses and https://www.rd.go.th/english/37741.html
- Stripe Checkout supports a separate one-time `payment` mode for credit-pack purchases. Stripe Billing Credit Grants are monetary credits scoped to metered billing Prices, so they do not directly represent this product's receipt-scan units. Sources: https://docs.stripe.com/api/checkout/sessions/create and https://docs.stripe.com/api/billing/credit-grant/create
- Stripe `invoice.paid` can also mean an invoice was marked paid out of band; card-paid Entitlements therefore require verification of the actual provider payment behind the invoice, unless a separate trusted manual Plan grant is explicitly recorded. Source: https://docs.stripe.com/api/events/types
- If the seller is VAT-exempt under Revenue Code section 81 or 81/1, a receipt is required immediately on each payment above THB 100 even without a customer request. All approved paid Plans and credit packs exceed that amount. Section 105 bis specifies seller tax ID, seller name, sequential receipt identification, issue date, amount, and five-year copy retention. Confirm the seller's actual status and format before live payment activation. Sources: https://www.rd.go.th/3435.html and https://www.rd.go.th/5203.html

## Release checks and deferred scope

- Verify in a Stripe test account that the chosen `unpaid` setting, 30-day recovery, outstanding-invoice payment, final cancellation, and new Checkout after cancellation do not create duplicate charges or accidentally revive an old billing period.
- Verify the seller's legal identity, tax status, exact receipt format, numbering, and delivery requirements with a Thai accountant before live checkout; VAT tax invoices remain out of scope while the seller lacks authority to issue them.
- Validate the Checkout, webhook event mapping, reconciliation, entitlement, and billing UI acceptance behavior specified in [phase-11-spec.md](./phase-11-spec.md).
- Document allowance implementation must make monthly grants, two-year expiry at the recorded Organization-local month boundary, earliest-expiring-first spending, downgrade clamping, failed-payment suspension/recovery, and concurrent intake atomic. Free does not accumulate, Trial does not transfer, and no prelaunch carry is credited.
- A firm buying Scan Credits on behalf of a Client Organization is outside the first version. The client owns and buys its own balance.
- Prove OCR accuracy, representative throughput/cost, and queue fairness before enabling Scan Credit sales; confirm a realistic peak pages/month per Organization before promising processing time for 5,000-page purchases.
