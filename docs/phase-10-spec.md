# Phase 10 — Accounting Firm Workspace and Multi-Client Export

Status: **specification synthesized from decisions confirmed on 2026-09-25; not implemented or activated**. This spec uses the [Phase 10 decisions](./phase-10-decisions.md), the terms in [CONTEXT.md](../CONTEXT.md), and [ADR 0020](./adr/0020-separate-entitlements-from-authorization.md), [ADR 0034](./adr/0034-model-accounting-firms-as-organizations.md), [ADR 0035](./adr/0035-require-client-grant-and-firm-assignment.md), and [ADR 0036](./adr/0036-split-firm-capabilities-from-client-resource-plans.md).

## Problem Statement

An accounting firm serving several businesses must switch Organization Context repeatedly to find missing documents, assign review work, track deadlines, and prepare approved data. The current Organization-scoped review and export flows cannot give the firm one authorized portfolio or one workbook across its clients. Simply adding a firm Plan or a client selector would risk exposing a Client Organization's records to staff whom that client did not authorize, and a stale aggregate or ready export could keep exposing data after access is revoked.

## Solution

Provide an Accounting Firm Organization with its own Plan, Memberships, staff seats, portfolio, workload view, Expected Document checklists, combined Review Queue, and on-demand daily briefing. A Client Organization remains the owner of its records and explicitly approves a scoped, expiring Firm Access Grant. The firm assigns its own staff to that client. Every delegated operation combines current firm entitlement and role, Client Assignment, client grant and action scope, tenant and record authorization; a Plan never supplies client authority. A client may authorize several firms independently.

An assigned firm Owner/Admin can explicitly select authorized clients and request one versioned XLSX containing a Summary sheet and one Approved-row sheet per client. The private artifact is built asynchronously with bounded memory and hard size limits. Request, generation, job access, and download recheck authorization and source revisions; any change that invalidates one included client invalidates the whole workbook. The original requester alone can download it while still authorized.

## User Stories

1. As a firm Owner, I want an Accounting Firm Plan on my existing Organization, so that my team can use firm capabilities without moving clients' data into our Organization.
2. As a Client Organization Owner, I want my Organization to retain ownership of its Documents and approvals, so that hiring a firm does not change the tenant boundary.
3. As a firm Owner/Admin, I want to request a relationship with a named Client Organization, so that the client can decide whether to authorize us.
4. As a client Owner/Admin, I want to inspect the firm's identity and requested scopes before approving, so that I know whom I am authorizing.
5. As a client Owner/Admin, I want recent reauthentication before granting, renewing, expanding, or revoking access, so that a stale session cannot change this sensitive relationship.
6. As a firm Owner/Admin, I want to accept a client-approved relationship and expanded scopes, so that access starts only after both sides agree.
7. As either party, I want to cancel a pending relationship, so that an unwanted request cannot remain open indefinitely.
8. As a client Owner/Admin, I want a grant to start with `read` only, so that review, approval, export, and original download each need explicit consent.
9. As a client Owner/Admin, I want to see that a read grant covers eligible current and future Documents, so that I understand the ongoing access I am approving.
10. As a client Owner/Admin, I want to reduce scopes or revoke a grant with immediate effect, so that the firm loses that authority even if its portfolio counters are stale.
11. As a firm Owner/Admin, I want to end a client relationship, so that my staff no longer access that client.
12. As a client Owner/Admin, I want grants to expire after 12 months unless renewed, so that access is reviewed periodically.
13. As an authorized grant manager on either side, I want a renewal reminder in the workspace beginning 30 days before expiry, so that the relationship does not lapse unexpectedly.
14. As a client Owner/Admin, I want to authorize more than one firm with separate scopes, rosters, expiry, and audit, so that one firm cannot inspect another firm's work.
15. As a firm Owner/Admin, I want to assign one or several active staff members to a granted client, so that I can distribute responsibility without adding each person as a client Membership.
16. As a client Owner/Admin, I want to inspect the assigned firm staff roster and its changes, so that I can see who may work on my data without approving every assignment.
17. As a firm staff member, I want client access to stop as soon as my Client Assignment, firm Membership, grant, or needed scope ends, so that old links and open tabs cannot extend access.
18. As a firm Owner/Admin who is not assigned to a client, I want to see only its relationship identity, grant state, and roster, so that I can manage assignments without seeing client work counters or records.
19. As a firm Member, I want the portfolio to show only clients assigned to me, so that the workspace does not reveal other relationships.
20. As a firm user assigned to a client, I want to preview eligible original Documents under `read`, so that I can verify work against the source.
21. As a client Owner/Admin, I want `original.download` separate from preview, so that downloading original bytes needs its own consent and fresh check.
22. As a firm Owner/Admin, I want a paginated portfolio with stable client ordering, so that I can manage several clients without missing rows when counters change.
23. As an authorized firm user, I want portfolio rows to show grant and Drive status, missing-item and review counts, assigned staff, and content-free activity, so that I can see where attention is needed without exposing document contents.
24. As an authorized firm user, I want a “needs follow-up” filter and the counter refresh time, so that I can prioritize work while understanding that counters may lag.
25. As a client Owner/Admin, I want to define monthly or quarterly Expected Document Templates, so that required documents can be tracked each accounting period.
26. As an authorized checklist editor, I want to edit Expected Document Items for a period and set due dates in the client's timezone, so that the checklist reflects that client's actual obligations.
27. As an authorized checklist editor, I want to link an accessible Document manually to an Expected Document Item, so that completion is based on a human decision.
28. As an authorized firm user, I want a pending item to become Missing/Overdue only after its due date without a linked Document, so that documents are not marked missing too early.
29. As a firm Owner/Admin, I want to assign missing-item follow-up to staff with the relevant Client Assignment and `checklist.manage`, so that workload has a responsible person.
30. As a firm staff member, I want my follow-up assignment removed when I lose client access, so that I cannot keep a client work item through an old assignment.
31. As an assigned firm reviewer, I want one cross-client actionable Review Queue with Mine, Unassigned, and Client filters, so that I can work across granted clients without changing Organization Context repeatedly.
32. As a firm Owner/Admin, I want to assign a Review Item only to staff who can currently review that client, so that item assignment cannot create access.
33. As a firm reviewer, I want opening a Review Item to reauthorize the current client, Document, and action, so that a stale queue row never grants entry.
34. As a client Owner/Admin, I want `review.propose`, `review.confirm`, and `suggestion.approve` to be separate scopes, so that proposals, extraction confirmation, and Accounting Suggestion approval are independently controlled.
35. As a firm reviewer, I want Task completion and queue assignment to leave confirmation and approval unchanged, so that only an explicit authorized human decision changes reviewed data.
36. As a firm Owner/Admin, I want per-staff workload counts for assigned actionable Review Items, overdue checklist follow-up, and open firm-owned Tasks, so that I can balance work without using money or upload volume as a productivity score.
37. As a firm user, I want an on-demand daily briefing in the firm workspace, so that I can see assigned reviews, client-local due and overdue items, open work, expiring grants, and failed exports.
38. As a firm user, I want the briefing day based on the firm's timezone and document due dates based on each client's timezone, so that deadlines have the intended local meaning.
39. As a client Owner/Admin, I want only my Owner/Admin peers to initiate or reauthorize our optional Google Drive Connection after recent authentication, so that firm staff cannot start OAuth for us.
40. As an assigned firm staff member, I want to ask the client to connect Drive and see its connection status, so that I can follow up without obtaining the client's provider credentials.
41. As a firm Owner/Admin, I want to select each client explicitly for a multi-client export, so that the default cannot accidentally include every authorized client.
42. As a firm Owner/Admin, I want a preflight count, date basis, Archived option, and template label, so that I know which Approved rows the request will contain.
43. As a firm Owner/Admin, I want export dates interpreted in each client's timezone, so that one date filter does not shift local accounting boundaries.
44. As a firm Owner/Admin, I want a Summary sheet identifying every selected client, timezone, range, sheet, row count, and creation time, so that the workbook can be checked without mixing financial totals.
45. As a firm Owner/Admin, I want one versioned Approved-row sheet per selected client, including a header-only sheet for a zero-row client, so that selection and omissions are unambiguous.
46. As a client Owner/Admin, I want only current Approved Export Rows included, so that raw OCR, drafts, unconfirmed values, Trash, and Purged Documents cannot enter a firm workbook.
47. As an export requester, I want large exports to run as background jobs with Queued, Running, Ready, Failed, and Expired states, so that I can understand their progress and safely retry failures.
48. As an export requester, I want a request over the client or row ceiling rejected before work starts and a byte-limit failure reported without truncation, so that I never mistake a partial workbook for a complete one.
49. As an export requester, I want the entire workbook invalidated if one grant, assignment, role, Document, or approval revision changes, so that a partial or stale file is never delivered silently.
50. As the original export requester, I want to download a Ready workbook only while its retention window and my current authority remain valid, so that possession of a link or job ID is not enough.
51. As a different firm Owner/Admin, I want to create my own export request rather than reuse another person's artifact, so that export provenance is clear.
52. As a client Owner/Admin, I want to inspect content-free audit for exports containing my Organization's rows without seeing other clients, so that I can investigate disclosure of my data.
53. As a firm Owner/Admin, I want to inspect firm grant, assignment, and export audit, so that I can investigate access without storing workbook cells in logs.
54. As a firm Owner, I want pending client requests and staff invitations to reserve limited slots atomically and release them when rejected or expired, so that concurrent requests cannot exceed the Plan.
55. As a firm Owner whose active Plan is over its 20-client or 5-seat ceiling after a downgrade, I want existing authorized work and recovery actions to continue while additions are blocked, so that a Plan change does not silently revoke client consent.
56. As a firm Owner whose Firm Plan has expired, I want to manage relationship status and security while new firm workspace work stops, so that I can revoke access and retrieve an already Ready, still authorized file within its retention window.
57. As a product operator, I want Phase 10 capabilities gated until their authorization and dependency tests pass, so that portfolio/checklist can be assessed separately from Phase 9-dependent review and export.

## Implementation Decisions

### Organization, Plan, and relationship lifecycle

- An Accounting Firm Organization is an existing Organization with its own Owner/Admin/Member Memberships and a distinct Accounting Firm Plan. Client Organizations remain separate tenants. Do not create a second user or firm membership system.
- The Firm Plan enables portfolio, delegated review, and multi-client Approved export. Its initial limit is 20 client relationships and 5 firm seats including Owner. Count active relationships/Memberships plus pending requests/invitations as reservations, with atomic admission checks. A pending firm-client request expires after seven days; the existing staff invitation lifetime remains 24 hours. Rejection, cancellation, revocation, and expiry release reserved capacity idempotently.
- When an active Firm Plan is over its new limit, reject new client requests and staff invitations that need another slot; an already reserved pending relationship may finish without consuming another slot. Keep already authorized work and recovery/security actions. When the Firm Plan itself expires, stop new portfolio, workload, briefing, delegated review, and export work. Continue relationship/status and security operations. A previously Ready artifact may be downloaded before its expiry only by its original requester after fresh client authorization. Never use a Plan state to override client access checks.
- Client Plans continue to govern that client's Document intake quota, OCR availability, and Drive. Phase 10 adds no shared document/OCR pool and no new commercial OCR quota. A Free client may authorize firm work. The delegated multi-client export uses the firm's entitlement; the existing single-client Phase 9 export keeps its client Plan and role checks.
- Relationship state is Firm request → Client approval → Firm acceptance → Active. The client Owner/Admin verifies firm identity and approves explicit scopes after recent reauthentication. Pending state reserves a firm slot but allows no client data access. Either party may cancel pending; either party may end Active. Client grant revocation requires recent reauthentication and takes effect immediately. An Active grant expires after 12 months without renewal. Expansion and renewal require fresh client Owner/Admin authentication; expanded scope also requires firm acceptance. Scope reduction is effective immediately. Show authorized managers on both sides a workspace reminder from 30 days before expiry; send no automatic LINE/email.
- A Client Organization may have grants to multiple firms. Enforce uniqueness and lifecycle per firm-client pair, without exposing another firm's grant, roster, audit, or work to the current firm.
- The first grant scope set is `read`, `review.propose`, `review.confirm`, `suggestion.approve`, `checklist.manage`, `export.approved`, and `original.download`; default to `read` only. A grant applies to eligible current and future client Documents, disclosed on the approval screen. Record access requires `read`; a delegated action also requires its own scope. `read` allows authorized preview; original-byte download additionally needs `original.download` and a fresh check. Firm Owner/Admin manages several staff assignments per client; a firm Member can be assigned to several clients. An accepted grant begins with no staff assigned. The client can view its current firm roster and change history.

### Authorization and tenant boundaries

- Keep direct Organization Context for client Membership access and a distinct Firm Client Context for delegated access. For every delegated list, detail, mutation, background job, and download, derive authority server-side from the authenticated user, current firm Membership and role, applicable Firm Plan capability while starting new work, current accepted/unexpired grant and scope, current Client Assignment, Client Organization ID, Document state, and record visibility. The only expired-Plan exception is download of an already Ready artifact under the separate current-client and retention checks above. The firm Plan, assignment, request-supplied IDs, cached counters, signed URL, and job ID are never authority on their own.
- An unassigned firm Owner/Admin can manage relationship identity, grant state, and roster only; client counters and records require that person's own Client Assignment and applicable scope. A firm Member sees only assigned clients. Filter aggregate reads by current grants and assignments before returning rows; open records through a fresh client/record check. Revocation, expiry, Membership loss, scope reduction, and assignment removal take effect immediately even if an aggregate read model is delayed.
- Client-owned review, confirmation, and approval stay tenant-scoped. Firm staff need the exact action scope and current review/approval revision in addition to firm access. Assigning review work cannot widen a grant. Task completion never confirms or approves. Phase 10 firm Review Item assignment is separate from the existing Client Organization Task Assignee model, which requires a Client Organization Membership. Removing a Client Assignment makes affected delegated review and checklist follow-up assignments ineffective/unassigned without completing the underlying work.
- Read, preview, original download, queue, checklist, export status, and download endpoints must avoid cross-tenant existence disclosure. Return safe unavailable/forbidden results without foreign filenames, Tax IDs, amounts, OCR text, notes, or workbook cells. Preserve CSRF protection on mutations, tenant-scoped database policies, least-privileged job access, and private artifacts.

### Firm workspace

- The portfolio read model contains client identity, grant and optional Drive state, assigned staff, content-free activity, missing Expected Document Item count, and actionable Review Item count. It contains no filename, Tax ID, amount, OCR text, correction note, or original content. Aggregate counters may lag at most five minutes and show their last-updated time; they cannot authorize actions.
- Page clients with a stable cursor, 10 per page, ordered by client name plus stable ID. Offer a “needs follow-up” filter; do not paginate by changing counter values. Build authorized client sets and counters using set-based indexed reads rather than one API or SQL round trip per client. Apply the same constraint to workload, briefing, and the combined Review Queue.
- Expected Document Templates are per client and monthly or quarterly. They instantiate editable Expected Document Items for an accounting period. Human actors with client Owner/Admin authority or assigned `checklist.manage` authority set due dates and manually link an accessible Document. Before the client-local due date an unlinked item is Pending; afterward it is Missing/Overdue. Do not infer a missing item or match a Document from OCR automatically. Firm Owner/Admin may assign follow-up only to firm staff with that client assignment and `checklist.manage`; this follow-up is separate from Client Assignment and Task Assignee.
- The combined Review Queue contains actionable rows from currently authorized clients. Use stable keyset pagination, a client label, and Mine, Unassigned, and Client filters. A row requires its applicable `review.propose`, `review.confirm`, or `suggestion.approve` scope. Firm Owner/Admin assigns only currently authorized staff; staff do not self-claim in the first version. Reauthorize on open, save, confirm, approve, return, and navigation. No bulk confirm or bulk approval.
- Per-staff workload counts assigned actionable Review Items, assigned overdue checklist follow-up, and open Tasks owned by the Accounting Firm Organization and assigned through its existing Membership model. It is not a leaderboard and does not use money or all uploads as effort. A firm Task may refer to an assigned client without copying protected client content; Phase 10 does not create a delegated Client Organization Task Assignee.
- The on-demand daily briefing uses the firm timezone for its day, each client timezone for due/overdue items, and only currently authorized, content-free review, missing-item, open-work, expiring-grant, and failed-export status. No automatic LINE/email delivery.
- A Google Drive Connection remains optional and per Client Organization, governed by that client's Plan. Only its Owner/Admin after recent authentication can start or reauthorize OAuth; firm staff can request a connection but cannot start OAuth, access provider tokens, or use one client's Drive for another client's data. The current Owner-only route must be reconciled with this Owner/Admin contract when implemented.

### Multi-client Approved workbook

- The request UI starts with no clients selected. Accept an explicit set of 1–20 distinct clients for which the requester is a current firm Owner/Admin, assigned, and granted `export.approved`. A firm Member cannot request or download bulk export. Use the firm Plan's export entitlement while still checking every client's grant, tenant, Document, and Approved-row authorization.
- The first format is a generic, versioned XLSX for onward handling, not a named accounting product import. Freeze selected clients, requester, filters, Archived choice, client-local date interpretation, Approved row IDs and source/approval revisions, template version, and per-client row counts at request time. Count and reject requests above 20 clients or 50,000 total Approved rows before enqueueing. Available Documents are included by default; Archived requires explicit opt-in; Trash and Purged never enter the workbook.
- Produce a Summary sheet with stable client identifier/name, client timezone, requested date range, deterministic distinct sheet name, Approved row count, and workbook creation time, without a cross-client monetary total. Produce one sheet per selected client, including a header-only sheet when its row count is zero. Client sheet columns and row identity match the versioned Phase 9 Approved contract (`accounting_suggestions_v1` initially); incompatible changes require a new multi-client template version. Treat names and cell data as untrusted text: no formulas, macros, executable links, or external references.
- Always use an asynchronous Durable Job and private Export Artifact. Page/stream authorized rows and stream workbook output with bounded memory. Enforce a 128 MiB XLSX byte ceiling during generation and a bounded job/concurrency policy; fail safely and delete unusable output rather than truncating. Show Queued, Running, Ready, Failed, and Expired with safe retry guidance. Do not write a multi-client workbook into any Client Organization Drive.
- At request, generation, completion, status, and download, verify the requesting identity, current firm role, every selected grant/scope and assignment, current row eligibility and source/approval revisions, and artifact lifecycle. A revoked or expired grant, lost assignment/role, inaccessible Document, or changed row revision invalidates the entire job/artifact; delete it and require a new request. Never quietly omit a client or substitute newer rows. A file already downloaded cannot be recalled.
- Retain an Export Artifact privately for at most 24 hours and issue a download link valid for at most 15 minutes. A link or job ID is only a locator. Only the original requester may obtain/download it while still a firm Owner/Admin assigned and export-authorized for every included client. An otherwise authorized colleague starts a new job. A Ready artifact may remain eligible after Firm Plan expiry only within these limits and current client authorization.

### Audit, performance, and activation

- Keep append-only, content-free grant, roster, assignment, review delegation, and export lifecycle audit for one year, subject to privacy/legal approval. Capture actor, firm/client IDs, scope and grant revisions, timestamps, safe transition/reason codes, request/job correlation, and per-client row counts. Include request, denial, completion, download, invalidation, and expiry. Do not log filenames, original/OCR content, correction values, workbook cells, tokens, or signed URLs.
- A Client Owner/Admin can inspect only audit facts concerning that Client Organization; a firm Owner/Admin can inspect firm audit under current firm authorization. Client audit of a multi-client export must not reveal other selected clients, their row counts, or their sheet names. Audit reads themselves obey current tenant and role checks.
- Performance targets at a representative 20-client/5-staff workload are P95 no more than two seconds for portfolio and combined queue reads and completion within ten minutes for a 50,000-row workbook. Measure query count, CPU, RAM, worker concurrency, bytes, row throughput, P50/P95, and projection freshness; a build or synthetic smoke test is not acceptance. Avoid client-by-client N+1 and unbounded in-memory row accumulation.
- Add Phase 10 behind default-off gates. Portfolio and checklist may activate after their own tenant, authorization, and performance proof. Delegated firm review and multi-client Approved export must remain off until Phase 9 OCR/review/export staging, security, privacy, and performance gates pass. Verify flag-off behavior, migration safety, and rollback before activation. Do not infer external readiness from deployment status alone.

## Testing Decisions

- The primary seam is the authenticated firm-client API journey: firm request → client approval → firm acceptance → staff assignment → portfolio/checklist/review → selected-client export request → Durable Job → authorized download or invalidation. Assert public responses, persisted relationship/review/export state, workbook contents, and audit effects, rather than testing helper implementation details. Reuse the existing Organization-scoped tenant, review, export, and job HTTP test patterns.
- Use database-backed integration tests where concurrency and tenant policy matter: simultaneous 20th/21st client requests or 5th/6th staff invitations, pending expiry and slot release, grant/assignment/scope changes during reads and jobs, multiple firms on one client, cross-tenant selectors, annual expiry, stale review revisions, and cleanup of delegated assignments. Prove that a firm Plan or assignment without client grant cannot read data.
- Test portfolio, workload, briefing, and queue through the authenticated API with firm Owner/Admin/Member, client Owner/Admin, assigned/unassigned staff, and two firms. Verify relationship-only rows for unassigned managers, no client counters for them, no foreign client discovery, five-minute projection freshness, stable cursor pages, and bounded query count as client count rises. Open a stale row after revoke and verify fresh denial.
- Test each grant scope independently: read preview versus original download, proposal versus confirmation versus suggestion approval, checklist mutation, and export. Verify every mutation rechecks current client scope and record revision. Verify Task completion, item assignment, and revoked client membership do not imply approval or access.
- Exercise the firm workspace at desktop and narrow widths: client selection starts empty, portfolio pagination/filter, due-date timezone labels, safe missing-item linking, queue filters and reauthorization error recovery, accessible briefing, Drive request/status, grant renewal, and safe job states. Keep a small end-to-end browser path for the critical Thai-document delegated review and export journey rather than mirroring component internals.
- Parse generated XLSX as a consumer would. Assert Summary fields, deterministic unique sheet names, one sheet per selected client including zero rows, exact Phase 9 Approved v1 header/order and row identity, Thai text, numeric/date representation, Archived opt-in, client-local date boundaries, formula neutralization, and absence of raw/OCR/private fields. Test 1, 20, and 21 selected clients; 0, 50,000, and 50,001 rows; 128 MiB boundary behavior; retries; cancellation; and bounded worker memory. A completed job must never contain a truncated or partial workbook.
- Exercise the same export API/job path through request, generation, status, and download with grant revocation, scope reduction, role/assignment loss, Document status changes, Approved revision changes, artifact expiry, signed-link expiry, and Firm Plan expiry. Verify whole-file invalidation and deletion, requester-only download, and content-free client-specific audit. Inspect logs, metrics, and artifacts for tokens, URLs, names, amounts, Tax IDs, OCR/original text, and cross-client leakage.
- Before activation, record representative staging measurements for P95 portfolio/queue latency, 50,000-row completion, CPU/RAM headroom, concurrency, query count, and projection lag. Keep OCR human-reference accuracy acceptance and Phase 9 gates separate from Phase 10 synthetic or local checks.

## Out of Scope

- Creating a separate Firm entity, moving Client Organization data into a firm tenant, or adding every firm staff member as a Client Organization Membership.
- Firm-wide document/OCR quota pooling, a new commercial OCR quota, public Firm Plan pricing, checkout, or a new trial.
- Client-owned Task assignment to firm staff, review self-claim, automatic checklist/document matching, OCR-based missing-document inference, and automatic approval.
- Automatic LINE/email briefing or renewal messages; firm-initiated client Drive OAuth; storing a multi-client workbook in client Drive.
- Raw metadata, merely Confirmed values, originals, or unapproved data in the multi-client workbook; a named accounting-system import adapter, ledger posting, tax filing, or guaranteed import compatibility.
- Retaining a downloadable workbook beyond 24 hours, recalling a previously downloaded file, silently dropping an unauthorized client, or presenting deployment success as security/performance acceptance.

## Further Notes

- The existing review/export paths are Organization-scoped and Phase 9 is implemented behind a default-off `REVIEW_ENABLED` gate; its staging, OCR, privacy, and representative performance gates remain prerequisites for Phase 10 delegated review/export. Phase 9's single-client export role/Plan contract remains unchanged.
- The current XLSX writer buffers a single-sheet row set, so the 50,000-row, multi-sheet, streaming requirement needs a bounded writer before activation. The existing Durable Job lease, private artifact, 24-hour retention, and 15-minute link lifecycle provide the preferred worker seam.
- The existing Task Assignee belongs to the Task's Organization. Keep firm-owned Tasks in staff workload and model firm Review Item and checklist follow-up assignment separately; do not use a Client Organization Task assignment to smuggle in client Membership or access.
- The agreed source decisions and domain ADRs are linked at the top. This spec does not itself authorize migration, deployment, feature activation, or a claim that the release gates have passed.
