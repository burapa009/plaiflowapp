# Phase 13 secretary ticket breakdown — proposed

Status: **draft for granularity and dependency review; individual tickets have not been published**. Source: [Phase 13 spec](./phase-13-spec.md) and [decisions](./phase-13-decisions.md). Each numbered item delivers one Secretary capability through its user flow, authenticated API, persistence where needed, and behavior tests. The company path comes first; Phase 10-dependent capabilities remain blocked until their sources and authority are proven. No ticket authorizes financial advice, automatic business action, external LLM transmission, or feature activation.

1. **Company daily Secretary Briefing.** Blocked by: none. Delivers an on-demand, asynchronous Briefing Snapshot for the current user and Organization-local day, with deterministic overdue/due-today ranking, up to five authorized actions, source-grounded reasons and links, remaining count, and a full-work link. Includes current-rights checks before gather, publish, display, and source open; duplicate opens share the same job/snapshot, snapshots expire, and the feature gate is off by default. The Phase 2 Assistant Summary keeps its existing contract.
2. **Briefing refresh and freshness.** Blocked by: 1. Delivers generation time, a possibly-stale indication for nonsecurity Task changes, manual refresh with the proposed 15-minute normal limit, and immediate hide/regeneration when rights change. A failed optional wording stage leaves the deterministic result available; job retries are bounded.
3. **“Why first?” Secretary Intent.** Blocked by: 1. Delivers a read-only explanation of the first authorized action's due group, Priority, and source, without reordering work or making financial recommendations.
4. **Contact follow-up Secretary Intent.** Blocked by: 1. Delivers “whom should I follow up with?” from human-marked, authorized ordinary Tasks and current responsible people. It does not send messages or create a separate Follow-up state.
5. **Meeting action capture and Intent.** Blocked by: 1. Delivers human-entered meeting name/date provenance on ordinary Tasks and a read-only view of open authorized meeting actions. Task permissions and completion remain authoritative; no calendar, transcript, or Meeting entity is introduced.
6. **Routine Templates.** Blocked by: none. Delivers Owner/Admin management of an Organization's weekly, monthly, and quarterly Routine Templates and responsible person, using the Organization Timezone and no holiday shifting.
7. **Routine Suggestions and human confirmation.** Blocked by: 1, 6. Delivers one visible Routine Suggestion when a template becomes due and an explicit authorized confirmation path to create an ordinary Task. Repeated or missed periods do not create a backlog or overlap an unfinished occurrence; no Task is created without confirmation.
8. **Briefing category visibility.** Blocked by: 1. Delivers per-Membership show/hide choices for authorized briefing categories without changing source records, another Membership, or access.
9. **Personal briefing pins.** Blocked by: 1. Delivers per-Membership pins that reorder only within the same due group, preserve source Task Priority, and disappear when the source is no longer authorized.
10. **Missing-document Secretary Intent.** Blocked by: 1; external dependency: implemented and authorized Phase 10 Expected Document Items and human Document linking. Delivers a read-only answer for client-local overdue missing items and their authorized follow-up owner. Before that source exists, the intent reports “not configured,” never “zero missing.” Completing a contact Task does not satisfy the item.
11. **Firm Secretary Briefing.** Blocked by: 1, 10; external dependency: implemented Phase 10 portfolio/review sources and proven delegated authorization. Delivers a firm-local daily briefing across only currently assigned and granted clients, with client-local document due dates, current grant/scope/record checks, safe revocation, and bounded aggregate queries.

## Shared acceptance for every ticket

- Each capability has an authenticated end-to-end API behavior and, where visible, a usable app path plus a test at that seam. A ticket is complete only when its behavior works through current Membership and record authorization; a Plan, snapshot, pin, job ID, or deep link is never authority.
- No source text, prompt, title, or briefing prose enters logs or metric labels. Source strings and generated wording are untrusted display text. No ticket sends original files, OCR text, payment data, secrets, or financial values to an external model.
- Secretary Intents are predefined and read-only. A missing or unconfigured source is distinguished from an authorized source with zero results. No ticket automatically creates Tasks, sends follow-ups, approves data, or offers financial, tax, investment, payment, or accounting advice; ticket 7 creates a Task only after explicit human confirmation.
- The company pilot remains behind a default-off feature gate. Before activation, collect current-rights and cross-Organization proof, browser and timezone checks, cached/fresh p95 under a recorded workload, queue and retry evidence, privacy review, exact build/environment, and rollback evidence. The proposed company-pilot limits in the decisions require confirmation before activation.

## Later decisions, not tickets in this breakdown

- A named external LLM provider, its privacy/retention review and numeric spend ceiling are required before any external wording call. The deterministic briefing is the initial deliverable; no provider integration is ready for an agent now.
- Outbound LINE/email briefing and paid-plan marketing remain outside Phase 13's initial scope. Phase 10-dependent tickets are listed so their blocking edges are visible, but they cannot begin until the named external dependencies are proven.
